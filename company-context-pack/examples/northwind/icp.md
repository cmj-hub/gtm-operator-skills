# ICP
Updated: 2026-10-17

## Segments
- Mid-market B2B software: 200 to 2,000 paying accounts, billing monthly or annually.
- Vertical software: software for a single industry, such as clinics or logistics, where churn comes in clusters.

## Firmographics
- Mid-market B2B software: 50 to 400 employees. North America and the UK. Billing in Stripe or Chargebee. Help desk in Zendesk or Intercom.
- Vertical software: Unknown: size, region, and stack were not given.

## Buying triggers
- A quarter where churn went up.
- A new head of customer success hired in the last 90 days.
- A finance request to forecast renewals.
- A recent price increase.

## Roles
- Feels the problem: head of customer success.
- Signs: the CFO or COO, when the plan is over a set amount.

## Disqualifiers
- Companies under 100 paying accounts: too little history to score.
- Consumer subscription apps.
- Companies with no billing system.
