# Voice
Updated: 2026-10-17

## Tone
Plain, direct, a little dry. Short sentences that say what the list is and what to do with it (drafted, confirm).

## Words we use
- the list
- accounts
- why this account

## Words we avoid
- synergy
- AI-powered
- revolutionary
- health score

## Sample lines
- Most churn is visible three weeks early. Nobody is looking at the right tickets.
- Here is the list. Here is why each account is on it. Go call them.
- Open the list. Start at the top account. Read why it is there. (drafted, confirm)
