# Company
Updated: 2026-10-17

## What we sell
Northwind Analytics reads billing and help desk data and flags subscription customers likely to cancel in the next 60 days. The customer success team gets a short list each Monday with the reason each account is on it.

## Who buys
The head of customer success or VP of customer success at a B2B software company with 200 to 2,000 paying accounts. Source: inputs/northwind-site.md (company size) and the founder (job title).

## Price model
A flat monthly fee, tiered by the number of accounts tracked. No per-seat charges. Annual plans get two months free.

## Stage
A team of eleven in Leeds and Austin, selling since mid 2023.
