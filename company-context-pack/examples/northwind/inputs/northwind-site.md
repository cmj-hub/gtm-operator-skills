# Northwind Analytics (fictional)

This is an invented company for demonstration. It is not a real business. Any resemblance is accidental.

## Home page

**Know which customers will leave before they tell you.**

Northwind Analytics reads your billing and support data and flags subscription customers who are likely to cancel in the next 60 days. Your customer success team gets a short list every Monday morning, with the reason each account is on it.

Built for B2B software companies with 200 to 2,000 paying accounts.

Pricing: a flat monthly fee based on how many accounts you track. No per-seat charges. Annual plans get two months free.

## How it works

1. Connect billing and your help desk. Setup takes about a week.
2. We score every account against its own history.
3. Your team gets a ranked list and a one-line reason for each account.
