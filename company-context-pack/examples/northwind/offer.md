# Offer
Updated: 2026-10-17

## Offers
- Pilot: 30 days on one cohort of accounts, free of charge, so the buyer sees the list before buying. For any qualified buyer.
- Monthly plan: the Monday list with a reason for each account, flat monthly fee tiered by accounts tracked.
- Annual plan: the monthly plan with two months free.

## Outcomes
- "I know where to spend my team's week."
- "I stop finding out about a cancel from the invoice."

## How it starts
The buyer sends a read-only export of billing and tickets for one cohort. Setup takes about a week.
